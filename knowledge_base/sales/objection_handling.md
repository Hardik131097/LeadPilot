# Objection handling playbook

## Objection: we manage fine with Excel
Acknowledge that Excel works at small scale. Point out that errors and time cost grow with headcount and locations, and that compliance changes are hard to track in spreadsheets. Offer a payroll health-check checklist rather than pushing a demo.

## Objection: we already have an HR vendor
Respect the existing relationship. Ask when the contract renews and what the team wishes worked better. Offer a side-by-side comparison closer to renewal time.

## Objection: no budget right now
Do not push. Offer a useful resource and ask permission to reconnect in the next budget cycle. Mention that pricing is per employee, so it scales with the business.

## Objection: data security concerns
Explain Indian data hosting, role-based access, audit logs and DPDP Act 2023 support. Offer to share the security document with their IT team.

## Objection: migration effort
Explain that implementation takes 3 to 4 weeks with a dedicated onboarding manager and that existing Excel or legacy data is imported.
