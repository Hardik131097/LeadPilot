# PayNest customer case studies
(Illustrative case studies for this academic project. Customers are fictional.)

## Logistics case study: national courier company, 700 employees
Industry: Logistics. Problem: payroll for warehouse and delivery staff was done in Excel, took 3 days every month, and attendance disputes were frequent. Result with PayNest: payroll time fell from 3 days to 6 hours and attendance disputes dropped by 40 percent after geo-fenced mobile attendance.

## Manufacturing case study: auto-components maker, 1,500 employees
Industry: Manufacturing. Problem: 600 contract workers across two plants and a labour-inspection notice for PF filing gaps. Result with PayNest: zero compliance penalties in the following 12 months and contractor PF/ESI tracked in one dashboard.

## Healthcare case study: multi-specialty hospital group, 2,000 staff
Industry: Healthcare. Problem: 24x7 nursing rosters managed on paper and frequent overtime errors. Result with PayNest: roster planning time cut by half and overtime pay calculated automatically from shift data.

## Retail case study: grocery chain, 60 stores
Industry: Retail. Problem: store managers sent attendance on WhatsApp and payroll needed manual consolidation. Result with PayNest: one dashboard for all 60 stores and payroll closed 2 days earlier each month.

## IT services case study: software services firm, 800 employees
Industry: IT Services. Problem: frequent payroll errors on variable pay and slow onboarding of new hires. Result with PayNest: payroll errors reduced by 90 percent and onboarding time cut from 2 days to 15 minutes per hire.

## Education case study: school group, 5 campuses
Industry: Education. Problem: seasonal teacher hiring and separate payroll spreadsheets for each campus. Result with PayNest: single payroll for all 5 campuses and new teachers onboarded before the academic term began.

## Fintech case study: payments company, 300 employees
Industry: Fintech. Problem: audit observations on HR record-keeping and labour-code readiness. Result with PayNest: complete audit trail for every HR change and labour-code updates applied automatically.
