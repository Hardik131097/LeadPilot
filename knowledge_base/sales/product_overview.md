# PayNest product overview
(PayNest is a fictional company created for this academic project.)

## Payroll automation
PayNest runs monthly payroll in a few clicks. It calculates salary, overtime, arrears, reimbursements and deductions, generates payslips automatically and emails them to employees. Payroll that takes HR teams 2 to 3 days in spreadsheets typically runs in under 4 hours.

## Statutory compliance (PF, ESI, PT, TDS, LWF)
PayNest computes Provident Fund, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS and Labour Welfare Fund for every state and generates challans and return files. Compliance rules are updated automatically when laws change, including the new labour codes. A compliance calendar alerts HR before every due date.

## Attendance, shifts and rosters
Mobile attendance with geo-fencing and selfie check-in for field, warehouse and store staff. Rotating shift rosters for 24x7 operations such as hospitals, plants and logistics hubs. Attendance flows directly into payroll, removing manual re-entry.

## Contract and multi-location workforce
Manage contract workers and contractor compliance alongside permanent staff. Supports multiple plants, branches, stores and campuses with location-wise reports.

## Onboarding and employee self-service
Digital onboarding with document collection and e-sign lets a new hire be set up in under 15 minutes. Employees use the mobile app to view payslips, apply for leave, and submit investment proofs.

## Integrations and data security
Exports to Tally and Zoho Books, and imports data from Excel. Data is hosted in Indian data centres with role-based access and audit logs, supporting compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023.

## Pricing and implementation
Pricing is Rs 80 to 120 per employee per month depending on modules. Implementation and data migration from Excel or a legacy system typically take 3 to 4 weeks, with a dedicated onboarding manager.
