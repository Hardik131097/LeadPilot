# Outreach brand voice and compliance policy

## Brand voice
Write like a helpful peer, not a salesperson. Be warm, specific and brief. Use the prospect's first name and refer to something real about their company. One idea per email and one clear question as the call-to-action.

## Claims and evidence
Only use figures and results that appear in the PayNest knowledge base. Never invent statistics, customer names or awards. Describe case-study results as what a similar customer achieved, not as a promise. Never use words like guaranteed, 100 percent, risk-free, best, cheapest or urgent.

## Length and format
Email body under 160 words and subject line under 60 characters. LinkedIn connection note under 300 characters. No attachments or links in a first-touch email.

## Consent and opt-out (DPDP Act 2023 and anti-spam good practice)
Contact only business email addresses for business purposes. Every first-touch email must include a simple opt-out line, for example: If this is not relevant, just reply no and I will not follow up. Honour opt-outs immediately.

## Human approval
No outreach is sent without approval by a human sales representative. Drafts flagged by the reviewer must be corrected by a human before approval.
